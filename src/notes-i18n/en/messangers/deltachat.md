Delta Chat is a messenger that runs on top of ordinary email, turning messages into chats. Its main advantage is that no phone number is needed to sign up, and conversations are protected by end-to-end encryption.

Here is a step-by-step guide to installing and setting up Delta Chat.

### 📥 Step 1: Install the app

Delta Chat is available on all major platforms. You can download it for free from official app stores or from the [developer's website](https://delta.chat/ru/download).

- **iOS / iPhone**: Via the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/delta-chat/id1459523234).
- **Android**: Via [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=chat.delta) or [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/app/com.b44t.messenger).
- **Windows**: Via the [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9pjtxx7hn3pk) or with the command `winget install 9PJTXX7HN3PK`.
- **macOS**: Via [Homebrew](https://formulae.brew.sh/cask/deltachat) (`brew install --cask deltachat`) or from the [official website](https://delta.chat/ru/download).
- **Linux**: Available as [Flatpak](https://flathub.org/apps/chat.delta.desktop) (`flatpak install flathub chat.delta.desktop`), AppImage, DEB, RPM and Snap.

A full list of download links can be found on the official Delta Chat page: [https://delta.chat/ru/download](https://delta.chat/ru/download).

### ⚙️ Step 2: Set up an account

After installation, you have two main ways to get started.

#### Method 1: Instant setup (recommended)

This is the fastest and easiest method; it does not require an existing email address.

1.  Open the app and tap **"Create a new profile"**.
2.  Enter the name or nickname your contacts will see.
3.  Tap **"Accept and create profile"**.

Done! The app will automatically create a random account on a Chatmail server for you. You don't need to configure anything, remember passwords or phone numbers — all credentials are stored securely on your device. You can learn more about Chatmail servers on the [project's official website](https://chatmail.at).

#### Method 2: Use an existing email account

If you want to use your current email address with Delta Chat:

1.  Open the app and choose **"Use another server"** or **"Sign in with email"**.
2.  Enter your email address and password.
3.  The app will automatically try to detect your mail server settings (IMAP/SMTP).

> **Important tip**: For some email providers (such as Gmail), you may need to create and use a special **"app password"** instead of your usual account password. Instructions for creating a Gmail app password can be found on [Google's help page](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/185833).

The developers also recommend creating a **separate email address** specifically for Delta Chat, so you don't mix regular mail with instant messages.

### 👥 Step 3: Add contacts

Once set up, you can start chatting in several ways:

- **Scan a QR code**: The most secure method. When you meet in person, scan each other's QR codes to exchange encryption keys.
- **Invite link**: Send your personal invite link through any other channel (SMS, another messenger, social networks).
- **By email address**: If you know someone's email address, you can message them directly. The message will arrive even if the recipient doesn't have Delta Chat installed.

More use cases and answers to frequently asked questions are collected in the [official Delta Chat documentation (FAQ)](https://delta.chat/ru/help).

### 💻 Using multiple devices

You can use the same Delta Chat account on your phone and computer. To do this:

1.  On the new device, select **"Add as second device"**.
2.  On the device where the account is already set up, go to **Settings → Add second device**.
3.  Scan the QR code that appears on the screen with the new device. Your messages and contacts will be synchronized.

If you need a more detailed guide, the full documentation is available on the [official Delta Chat wiki](https://deltachat.wiki).
