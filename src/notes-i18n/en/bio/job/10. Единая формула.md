# The Unified Formula

date: 2026-11-24

> Author's position: work is the most interesting thing; education fits too, but its goal is to organise something in the industry, while learning is a continuous process. The repository is an open hub of everything created, a node around which a network and connections are built. Sponsorship is understood as "becoming part of" something, not as begging for funds.

## The Formula

```
Anchor = (W + E + H) × L − C
subject to: Income ≥ stable threshold
```

Where:

- **W — work.** Not just money: applying a skill, recognition, a point of growth.
- **E — education.** Not a diploma as a trophy, but a way of organising knowledge in the industry. Continuous learning is the fuel, not a stage that ends.
- **H — hub.** An open repository where everything created stays visible, connected and reusable.
- **L — legal anchor.** A visa or sponsorship: the multiplier that turns a set of facts into a legal status.
- **C — cost of compromises.** Climate, scale of the country, distance, visa risks, giving up something desirable.
- **Income** is not a term in the sum but a constraint: if it is unstable, the formula does not work.

## Why the hub is not just a third term

The hub does two jobs at once.

1. **Interface.** It turns W and E into evidence: a commit, a release, a note, a changelog, a developer letter. Without a hub, work and learning remain invisible to the immigration system.
2. **Assembly point.** Facts from W and E are collected in H, and only then can they be multiplied by L. The order matters: first the artifact, then the legal operation.

That is why the hub is the only element that survives a change of country, employer and visa. Everything else changes; what has been accumulated remains.

## The network: who is connected to whom

The author's idea (the first one after reading): if everyone takes part, a whole system of connections can be set up. This is not just a metaphor — it is a working model:

- **The hub is a node.** W, E and Income are properties of the node.
- **Connections are edges.** A mention, a review, a referral, an expert letter, a joint release.
- **Sponsorship is an edge with legal weight.** It does not merely connect nodes; it changes the status of one of them.
- For an O-1 advisory opinion — an edge from an expert to your node. For NIW — expert letters. For hiring — a referral from a company employee.

So "becoming part of" means embedding the node in a network, not simply getting a visa. And a network can only be built around a node that contains artifacts: an empty node has nothing to connect.

## What sponsorship is

Sponsorship is not "the employer whisked you away somewhere" and not charity. It is **one party's commitment to take legal responsibility for your presence in exchange for your labour**.

Roles change depending on the visa:

| Route | Who becomes the anchor | What the anchor provides | What you provide |
|---|---|---|---|
| H-1B | Employer | LCA, I-129 petition, promised salary | Work in the stated role |
| O-1A | Employer or agent | Petition, expert letter, contract, return airfare on termination | Work in the field of recognition |
| L-1 | Corporate group | Intra-group transfer | You are already part of the group abroad |
| EB-2 / EB-3 | Employer | PERM or job offer, green card | Permanent employment |
| EB-2 NIW | Your own endeavor | Self-petition, no employer required | Developing your own project in the US |
| F-1 | Educational institution | Student status | Study and tuition |
| E-2 | Your investment | Investor status | Capital and management |

So the author's intuition is right: **the point is to become part of something**. Moving is just a side effect of being attached. And this is not "abuse, begging for funds": an employer sponsors because it needs a specific skill, and pays with money, time and risk. You do not arrive with an empty request but with a hub of accumulated work — the exchange has already happened before anything is signed.

## Testing the formula with zeros

- **H = 0** → L is almost unattainable: there is nothing to present.
- **L = 0** → the sum exists but is not legally attached to anything; all that remains is remote work from Russia.
- **W = 0** → no income, the formula does not work.
- **E = 0** → the hub stops being replenished and gradually becomes outdated.
- **C is too large** → compromises eat the result: the country is there, but there is no life.

## Practical order

1. **H** — a public repository, 3 cases, an honest README.
2. **W** — income and recognition that flow into the hub.
3. **L** — an anchor for a specific offer, not "just in case".
4. **Geography** — last, as a consequence of the anchor.

Education here is not a competitor to work: it is a source of artifacts for the hub. You can keep learning continuously — what matters is that the learning leaves a trace.
