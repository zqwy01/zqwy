For those for whom small occurrences are more acceptable than gigantic ones

Indeed: why read, for example, "War and Peace", when you can easily declare it "outdated".
Living in nostalgia is by no means a solution to the problem of inheritance; it is merely a memory of the past, like a poisonous sweetness that conveys only one thought: what came before will never return. Yet with this assertion one can claim that one agenda will give way to another. And the problem is precisely that this is like a pendulum, rather than a solution that builds a balance — the very balance that creates that harmony in which each element takes its own place in accordance with the reality of heritage, not with the unreality of an imagined justice that for some reason is enacted at someone else's loss. For the reality is that the longing is not for revenge, but for justice.
The question is who will be the first to abandon the language of retribution in favor of the language of recognition — and that is no longer an aesthetic question but a political one, which makes it much slower, yet all the more rewarding to build and learn from.

And all this thanks to the device, the network, the infrastructure devised by specific people of a specific culture, a specific upbringing and a specific origin. This does not mean that those others are worse or better: what makes them different is precisely that they cannot understand how all of this exists and be grateful for it.
