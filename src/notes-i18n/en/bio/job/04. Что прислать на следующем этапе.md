This is a checklist of input data. Until it's available, any specifics about vacancies, salary and country remain guesswork.

## A. Required to choose a role and a country

- [ ] **City and time zone in the Russian Federation.** It isn't stated directly in the repository; `TZ=Europe/Moscow` is only a hint.
- [ ] **Whether the wish to relocate still exists at all.** Two scenarios are possible: remote work only from Russia with good foreign-currency pay, or an actual move. These are different strategies.
- [ ] **A new region or at least 3 countries instead of the rejected ones.** Georgia, Armenia, Turkey, the UAE, Kazakhstan, Serbia and the EU are off the table. Still allowed: Asia, Latin America, the Middle East excluding the UAE, Africa, Oceania, the UK/Switzerland/Norway/Iceland with caveats.
- [ ] **Role priority:** frontend/full-stack, DevOps/platform, web audio/media, or a mixed form with an explicit main choice.
- [ ] **Commercial experience:** companies, roles, dates, what was done, what results can be measured. If there is no commercial experience, say so directly; then we'll focus on projects and internships.
- [ ] **Education:** institution, specialisation, years, whether there is a degree.
- [ ] **English level:** I understand documentation / write / speak in interviews. A sample text or a short message works too.
- [ ] **Financial position:** current income, desired income, currency, how many months you can search without income.
- [ ] **Constraints:** health, family, animals, equipment, documents, relocation budget.

## B. For the portfolio and CV

- [ ] A GitHub link or permission to create a public repository with a demo branch.
- [ ] The current CV file, if there is one.
- [ ] Permission to publish case studies from a private project, or a note that only screenshots/descriptions may be shown.
- [ ] 2–3 vacancies you like and 2–3 that are absolutely unsuitable. They can be used to derive precise filters.
- [ ] Any external links worth adding to `/links/`, provided they don't obscure the technical profile.

## C. What I'll do once I have it

1. Put together a CV in RU and EN for the chosen anchor role, without "universal specialist".
2. Write 3 GitHub case studies with concrete numbers and file paths from `02. Портфолио и резюме.md`.
3. Prepare text for LinkedIn and a short letter for direct outreach to music-tech and self-hosted teams.
4. Compile a list of 20–30 vacancies in the chosen country/region and a separate list of Russian remote vacancies as a backup income source.
5. Lay out a 4-week plan: portfolio → applications → interviews → relocation decision.

## D. Minimal quick start

If you don't want to answer everything at once, four things are enough:

1. a city in the Russian Federation;
2. a new country/region not on the rejected list, or "staying in Russia, remote only";
3. experience: commercial or not, and how many years;
4. role choice: frontend/full-stack or DevOps/platform.

That's enough to make a first concrete CV draft and refine the list of platforms.
