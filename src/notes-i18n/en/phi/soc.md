Socialization is not the same as communicativeness.
The term "socialization" comes from civil-law and political process.

However, if you go through an orphanage, kindergarten, boarding school, camp, school, university, army, prison and so on, you can identify enough of these circles; sometimes they are institutional, and each of them can be included under the term 《socialization》.

The reality is: a person does not become capable of communication as a result.
Time spent in each of these places makes one's idea of the human world narrower and more down-to-earth. The time spent in these places does not improve a person, for every minute without free will is a poisoned minute;

Still less is it necessary to experience all the characteristic features of these places first-hand;

One can claim that socialization achieves specific benefits, but it is worth noting, with resonance, that these benefits do not necessarily correspond to: aesthetics, ethics and culture, including theology;

And here is the most important part of this factual formulation: socialization does not determine the moral side of the subject. Morality is instilled by experience, not by testing in an artificial environment.

This does not mean that exceptional and unique experience alone matters, but personality formation is not an obligation — it is just one interpretation of "friend/foe".

Therefore, the exchange of experience is about how a person builds communication.
