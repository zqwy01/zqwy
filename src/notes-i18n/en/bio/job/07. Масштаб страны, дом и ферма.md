# Country scale, own house and farm

date: 2026-11-24

> Supplement dated 24.11.2026: the list of destinations has been expanded — the USA, Mongolia, Greenland, northern China and others are covered in `08. Расширенный список крупных стран.md`. The filter logic and the breakdown of Australia/Canada/UK remain below.

Refinement from the author: what matters is not the metropolitan area, but **the size of the state as a whole**. A large city is only infrastructure, not a lifestyle. The lifestyle: own house, land/farm, pets. Alcohol culture has been removed as a filter: "pubs are their thing, we have that too; if it were banned, I wouldn't object."

## Active filters

1. A large country comparable to Russia in area.
2. The possibility of owning a house with land and keeping animals.
3. Moderate climate, without intense heat or bright sun.
4. An English-speaking environment is preferred; the EU is still excluded.
5. No federal legalization of recreational cannabis.
6. Stable income — more important than the romance of moving.

Conclusion: under the new filter **the UK ceases to be the main candidate**. It's good as remote work for a British company and as a language environment, but not as a country to live in with a farm.

## Revisiting Australia

Australia was previously rejected with the words "too hot". That's true for Queensland, the Northern Territory and the interior, but not for the south:

- Tasmania — cool temperate climate with four seasons ([Climate of Tasmania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_of_Tasmania)); the temperature regime is described as mild, without extreme heat ([Tasmanian Grown](https://tasmaniangrown.au/about-tasmanian-grown/)).
- The cool temperate zone includes most of Victoria and Tasmania, plus Canberra and part of inland NSW ([climate zone map](https://ultimatebackyard.com.au/australia-climate-zone-map/)).
- Hobby farms in Tasmania are a common and growing format; there is official support for both housing and agriculture ([Migration Tasmania](https://www.migration.tas.gov.au/live_and_work/live_in_tasmania), [ABC](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2021-10-14/interest-in-tasmanian-small-farms-growing-/100520032)).

On cannabis: recreational use is banned federally; the exception is the ACT, where since 2020 small-scale possession and cultivation have been permitted under territorial law; medical cannabis has been legal since 2016. So **Tasmania and Victoria pass the filter**, Canberra/ACT only partially.

Downsides of Australia: great distance from Russia, a time difference of about 7–8 hours with Moscow, so remote work for European teams is inconvenient — you need an APAC focus, async work or a local employer. Immigration via skilled migration and state nomination; Tasmania has its own nomination programme, which fits logically with the farming scenario. Conditions and thresholds need to be checked at the time of application.

## Final ranking

1. **Stay in Russia + foreign-currency remote work.** The only option that fully preserves the country's scale, house, land and animals while providing stable income. If desired, the move can be postponed until a new offer is clear.
2. **Australia — Tasmania or Victoria.** The best relocation option by the combination of scale, climate, a house with land and the absence of legalized cannabis. Requires an APAC job-search strategy and a visa/nomination.
3. **Canada.** Best of all on scale and farms, but federal cannabis legalization since 2018 directly conflicts with the filter. Drop it unless the filter is softened.
4. **United Kingdom.** Keep only as a source of remote income and language practice, not as a place for a farm.
5. **New Zealand, Switzerland, Norway, Iceland.** Fail on country scale.

## What this changes about the job search

- The remote search shifts toward **APAC/async-friendly** companies if Australia is considered; for the Russia scenario, UK/EU clients and the Russian market remain.
- Profile wording until the decision: `Remote from Russia (UTC+3), open to long-term relocation to a large non-EU country with a house and land`.
- The resume should explicitly mention experience with self-sufficiency, animals, house and equipment — for a farming/self-sufficient scenario that is as much a proof asset as code.
